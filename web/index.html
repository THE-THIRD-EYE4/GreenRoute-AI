    Relay K1 → ignitionCuts the key line when you ask
    Buzzer BZ1Sounds on the vehicle itself

    Power: the vehicle battery feeds a 5.4 V buck converter. An 18650 backup cell (TP4056 charger + 5 V boost) keeps the ESP32, buzzer and SIM800L running when the vehicle battery is removed. The SIM800L runs straight from the 18650 cell (3.7–4.2 V), never from 5 V.

    When someone removes the battery
    1. The INA226 sees the battery voltage below 6 V for 0.3 s (three readings in a row).
    2. The ESP32, still running on the 18650 backup, sounds the buzzer and logs the alert.
    3. This dashboard shows the warning band and plays the alarm tone.
    4. The SIM800L sends an SMS with a Google Maps link to the owner.
    5. You press Silence alarm. Reconnecting the battery is logged too.
    Cutting the ignition
    1. You press Cut ignition and enter the control PIN.
    2. The tracker checks the GPS speed. Above 5 km/h the cut is queued until the vehicle stops.
    3. The relay energises and its NC contact opens the key line. The tile shows Ignition cut.
    4. The cut is remembered after a reboot and re-applied once the speed check passes.
    5. A dead tracker never stops the vehicle: with no power the NC contact stays closed.
    How to connect
    1. Power the tracker. The Serial Monitor (115200) prints its IP address.
    2. Put your phone or laptop on the same 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and open http://<IP> or http://evtracker.local.
    3. No router nearby? Join the EV-Tracker hotspot (password evtracker123) and open http://192.168.4.1.
    4. For an app-like icon, use Add to Home screen in your phone's browser menu.
    Good to know
    1. The status refreshes every second. After 6 s with no reply the values dim.
    2. The street map needs internet. Without it you get an offline track plot.
    3. Browsers only play the alarm sound after you press Sound alerts on once.
    4. The SIM800L is 2G only: use Airtel, Vi or BSNL. Jio will not work.
